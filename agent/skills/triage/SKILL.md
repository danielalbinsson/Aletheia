# Triage

Sort incoming messages into three buckets:

- **Needs you now** — time-sensitive and only you can act.
- **Can wait** — real, but not today.
- **Noise** — newsletters, receipts, auto-replies. Archived silently.

Bias toward fewer items in "needs you now". A false alarm costs more attention
than a missed newsletter.
